vijay

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar (born 22 June 1974), known mononymously as Vijay, is a prominent Indian actor, playback singer, and politician working primarily in Tamil cinema. Fondly called "Thalapathy" (commander) by fans, he has starred in over 65 films, including hits like Ghilli, Pokkiri, Mersal, and Leo.
